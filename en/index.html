DunckOps Self-Hosted

Install your DunckOps stack on a VPS

This domain distributes the official self-hosted installer. Run the command below on your VPS and configure your license when you first sign in. If you do not provide a key, a free license will be configured automatically.

View plans on the official website Sign in
curl -fsSL https://get.dunckops.com/install.sh | sudo bash
1. Run on your VPS The installer downloads everything to /opt/dunckops and starts Postgres, MinIO, API, worker, web, and Docker Agent.
2. Sign in to your account Use your DunckOps account to sign in to the self-hosted instance.
3. Configure your license Enter your key or leave it blank to activate the free plan.
install.sh docker-compose.prod.yml Docker Ops override env.production.example dunckops.com

Requirements: Docker 24+, Docker Compose v2+, and a DunckOps account. Default update command: cd /opt/dunckops && ./scripts/update.sh.
